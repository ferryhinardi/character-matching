### 3. 🔥 Combo System

**How it Works:**
- Match multiple pairs consecutively without mistakes
- Build combo multiplier (3x, 4x, 5x, etc.)
- Earn bonus points for each combo level

**Combo Bonuses:**
- **3x Combo:** +100 points
- **4x Combo:** +200 points
- **5x Combo:** +300 points
- **Speed Bonus:** +200 points (match found < 3 seconds)

**Visual Feedback:**
- Large combo notification on screen
- Combo counter in stats panel
- Special sound effects for combos

**Strategy Tips:**
- Focus on matches you're confident about
- Speed matters - fast matches give speed bonus
- One mistake resets your combo!

---

### 4. ⚡ Power-Ups System

Two powerful abilities to help you win!

#### **💡 Hint (3 per game)**
- **What it does:** Reveals two matching cards
- **Cost:** -500 points
- **Duration:** Cards highlighted for 2 seconds
- **Keyboard:** Press `H`

**When to Use:**
- When you're stuck
- To maintain combo streak
- Near the end of a close game

#### **❄️ Time Freeze (2 per game)**
- **What it does:** Stops timer for 10 seconds
- **Cost:** -300 points
- **Visual:** Snowflake indicator shows remaining time
- **Keyboard:** Press `F`

**When to Use:**
- In Time Attack mode
- When timer is red (< 30 seconds)
- To think strategically

**Power-Up Strategy:**
- Use hints early to build combos
- Save time freezes for emergencies
- Balance points lost vs. time saved

### 5. 🎯 Challenge Modes

Four exciting game modes with unique rules!

#### **Classic Mode** (Default)
- **Rules:** Standard gameplay
- **Time Limit:** None
- **Move Limit:** None
- **Best For:** Learning and practicing

#### **Time Attack Mode** ⏱️
- **Rules:** Beat the clock!
- **Time Limits:**
  - Easy: 2 minutes
  - Medium: 3 minutes
  - Hard: 5 minutes
- **Penalty:** Lose if time runs out
- **Best For:** Speedrunners and thrill-seekers

#### **Limited Moves Mode** 🎯
- **Rules:** Careful with your moves!
- **Move Limits:**
  - Easy: 20 moves
  - Medium: 30 moves
  - Hard: 45 moves
- **Penalty:** Lose if moves exceeded
- **Best For:** Strategic players

#### **Memory Master Mode** 🧠
- **Rules:** Cards flip back faster (0.5s instead of 1s)
- **Time Limit:** None
- **Move Limit:** None
- **Best For:** Experts seeking a challenge

**How to Select Mode:**
- Level select screen → Game Mode dropdown
- Mode saved automatically
- Applies to all difficulties

## 🎯 Scoring System (Enhanced)

### **Base Formula:**
```
Score = 10,000
      - (moves × 100)
      - (time × 10)
      - (power-up penalties)
      + (combo bonuses)
```

### **Power-Up Penalties:**
- Each Hint used: -500 points
- Each Time Freeze used: -300 points

### **Combo Bonuses:**
- Combo multiplier × 100 points
- Speed bonuses: +200 points each

### **Examples:**

**Perfect Easy Game (No Power-Ups):**
- 8 moves (perfect), 30 seconds
- Base: 10,000
- Move penalty: -800
- Time penalty: -300
- **Final Score: 8,900** 🏆

**Good Medium Game (1 Hint, 3x Combo):**
- 15 moves, 60 seconds, used 1 hint, 3x combo
- Base: 10,000
- Move penalty: -1,500
- Time penalty: -600
- Hint penalty: -500
- Combo bonus: +300
- **Final Score: 7,700** 🌟

**Time Attack Hard (Survived!):**
- 40 moves, 295 seconds (barely made it!)
- Base: 10,000
- Move penalty: -4,000
- Time penalty: -2,950
- **Final Score: 3,050** 👏
